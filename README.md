# NO AI Plane Unstuck
Nuclear Option mod to expedite AI taxiing planes to take off faster, and get unstuck if they're stuck in a deadlock.
Functions (configurable):
- Force disembark planes that are stuck taxiing (haven't moved X distance in Y time, by default 5 meters in 30 seconds) so that they don't just indefinitely stay there in a deadlock (vanilla IsStuck in most cases doesn't end up being called as its timer gets reset for various reasons making it highly ineffective)
- Change the range a landing plane has to be from runway to mark it as blocked and cause AI to wait for the plane to land first before going on the runway, this is ~2500m in vanilla which can be overly far, default config here puts it to 500m
- Change obstacle avoidance cone of taxiing planes (only for the ones that haven't taken off yet), vanilla is 180 frontal degrees, plugin default sets it to 120 degrees so they're not as keen on stopping for more unrelated things on the side
- By default plugin sets aggressive take-off priority for the plane #1 in take-off list, so that (unless it needs to wait for a landing plane) it commits to going to the runway instead of waiting for another plane approaching from another converging taxiway. Plugin also fixes where AI planes that brake for anything also drop their position in queue, which can result in a loop where two or more planes that meet from converging taxiways are stuck in a loop where one is about to take-off, brakes for the other, gives up its slot, the other gets the next slot, but also brakes, gives up its slot and so on

In addition to those taxiing behaviours, plugin also allows adjusting settings related to where AI planes are deployed:
- Toggle this functionality with Enable Alternate Airbase Spawn (on by default)
- When this is on, a certain % of planes (based on rank) prefer diverting from the primary airbase (vanilla marks one airbase closest to a perceived threat/objective and exclusively tries deploying planes there as long as there's any space in it) to a secondary, least congested airbase. This by default is set to low % for low rank planes (only 5% for rank 0 and 25% for rank 1/2), and higher for higher rank planes with especially a high 90% for rank 4-6 planes, so that slower planes get more space to spawn at closest airbase, while high ranked, faster (with often longer ranged weaponry) planes spawn from further away as they mind the distance far less (and can often even utilise their weapons better), this greatly spreads load across all available airbases and adds variety of where AI can come from
- Increase how many AI planes are deployed at once, by default up to 3 instead of vanilla's one per tick (there's a tick every 5 seconds), with extra attempts (by default 5) when there's a larger backlog of planes to spawn, and only when there's an airbase with at most (by default) 4 planes on it at the moment
